# PRD

# 1. Product Overview

### Product Name

**Verba**

### Tagline

Discover Books. Understand Your Reading. Understand Yourself

### Product Vision

Verba is an AI-powered Reading Intelligence Platform that helps readers discover books aligned with their emotions and goals, organize their reading journey, and gain personalized insights from their reading history.

Unlike traditional reading platforms that focus on cataloging books or displaying reviews, Verba focuses on understanding the reader. By combining reading history, emotional feedback, and AI-driven analysis, Verba delivers highly personalized recommendations and meaningful reading insights.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Modern reading platforms such as Goodreads and StoryGraph have significantly improved how readers organize their libraries, track progress, and discover new books. They provide valuable reading statistics, recommendations, and goal-tracking features that help users manage their reading habits.

However, these platforms primarily present **reading data**, leaving readers to interpret it on their own. They answer questions like *what books were read*, *how many pages were completed*, and *which genres were most popular*, but offer limited support in understanding the deeper context behind those patterns.

As a result, many readers still struggle with questions such as:

- Why do I consistently enjoy certain books but abandon others?
- How have my reading interests evolved over time?
- Which emotions influence my reading choices?
- What should I read next based on both my past reading behavior and my current mindset?
- How can I build a reading habit that aligns with my personal goals?

Without these insights, readers often experience decision fatigue when choosing their next book, lose momentum in their reading habits, purchase books they never finish, and miss opportunities to intentionally broaden their knowledge or explore new interests.

This creates an opportunity for a platform that transforms reading data into personalized intelligence. By combining reading history, emotional reflections, goals, and AI-driven analysis, Verba helps readers not only discover books but also understand their evolving reading habits and make more intentional reading decisions.

# 4. Opportunity

Reading platforms have evolved beyond simple book catalogs. Applications such as Goodreads and StoryGraph help users organize their libraries, track reading progress, participate in challenges, and discover new books through recommendations and detailed reading statistics.

However, these platforms primarily focus on **what users read**, rather than **what their reading reveals about them**.

Readers increasingly use books for diverse purposes, including:

- Personal growth
- Mental well-being
- Career development
- Academic learning
- Entertainment
- Skill development

Despite this shift, there is still an opportunity to create a platform that understands the **context behind a reader's choices**. Questions such as:

- *Why does this reader enjoy certain books?*
- *How have their interests evolved over time?*
- *How do their emotions influence what they choose to read?*
- *What books align with both their current mood and long-term goals?*

remain largely unanswered by existing platforms.

Advances in AI, natural language processing, and behavioral analytics make it possible to move beyond traditional recommendation systems by incorporating:

- Reading history and preferences
- Current mood and reading intent
- User reflections and emotional responses
- Reading goals and evolving interests

This creates an opportunity to build an **AI-powered Reading Intelligence Platform** that not only recommends books but also helps readers better understand their reading habits, interests, and personal growth over time.

# 10. Core Features

## 1. Reading Data Import & Account Connections

Allow users to import their existing reading data through files or connect supported third-party accounts during onboarding.

**Import Reading Data**

- Goodreads CSV
- StoryGraph Export
- Generic CSV

**Connect Accounts**

- Google Books
- Notion
- Hardcover

Users may also start fresh and build their reading history manually.

The imported data and user-authorized account connections are analyzed to create an initial reading profile and improve recommendations from the first day of use. Available data depends on the permissions and capabilities supported by each service.

**Priority:** Must Have

## 2. AI-Powered Personalized Recommendations

Recommend books using a combination of:

- Reading history
- Reading goals
- Current mood
- Emotional reflections
- Previous ratings
- Reading behavior

Unlike traditional recommendation systems, recommendations continuously improve as users interact with the platform.

**Priority:** Must Have

## 3. Mood & Intent-Based Book Discovery

Allow users to discover books using natural language and emotional context rather than relying only on genres or authors.

Example searches:

- "I need something comforting."
- "Recommend a book for learning Product Management."
- "I want an inspiring biography."

**Priority:** Must Have

## 4. Digital Bookshelf & Reading Tracker

Provide a unified reading library where users can:

- Organize books
- Track progress
- Record reading sessions
- Manage reading status
- Monitor goals and challenges

Supports physical books, ebooks, and audiobooks.

**Priority:** Must Have

## 5. AI Book Insights

Provide concise AI-generated content for every book, including:

- Summary
- Key themes
- Important takeaways
- Reading difficulty
- Why the book is recommended

Designed to help users make informed reading decisions without replacing the original book.

**Priority:** Should Have

## 6. Emotion-Based Reflection

After completing a book, users can record:

- Overall rating
- Emotional response
- Personal reflection
- Favorite takeaway

These reflections become part of the user's evolving Reading Intelligence profile and improve future recommendations.

**Priority:** Must Have

## 7. Reading Goals & Challenges

Enable users to:

- Set annual or monthly reading goals
- Track books, pages, or reading time
- Join themed reading challenges
- Monitor progress through personalized dashboards

**Priority:** Should Have

## 8. AI Reading Wrapped

Generate personalized reading reports every three or six months featuring:

- Books completed
- Pages read
- Reading consistency
- Favorite genres and themes
- Emotional reading trends
- Reading style
- Top books
- AI-generated narrative summarizing the user's reading journey

**Priority:** Should Have

# 11. Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Limited support for third-party integrations | Users may not be able to import reading history from every platform | Initially support official exports (Goodreads, StoryGraph, CSV) and expand integrations over time |
| Cold-start problem | New users receive weaker recommendations | AI onboarding questionnaire combined with mood, goals, and genre preferences |
| Recommendation quality | Poor recommendations may reduce trust and engagement | Continuously refine recommendations using reading history, ratings, emotional reflections, and user feedback |
| Copyright restrictions | Cannot display or distribute copyrighted book content | Display book metadata and AI-generated summaries only; never reproduce copyrighted text |
| AI hallucinations | AI-generated summaries or recommendations may contain inaccuracies | Ground outputs using trusted book metadata and clearly identify AI-generated content |
| User privacy | Reading history and reflections are personal data | Require explicit consent for imports and process only user-authorized data |
